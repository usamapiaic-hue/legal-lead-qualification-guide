# Legal Lead Qualification Guide

Every law firm that markets online gets calls and forms from people it cannot help: the wrong practice area, the wrong state, a case that is already over, or no case at all. Qualifying those leads quickly and politely protects the attorney's time and still leaves a good impression. Published by Legal Web Agency.

## Qualify on four questions

Most intake teams can sort a lead with four questions, asked in the first two minutes.

| Question | What it tells you |
|----------|-------------------|
| What happened, in a sentence? | Practice area and whether there may be a case |
| When did it happen? | Whether deadlines may already have passed |
| Where did it happen / where do you live? | Whether the firm is licensed and serves that area |
| Has another lawyer worked on this? | Conflicts and existing representation |

Add one or two practice-specific questions (for example, "Were you injured and did you see a doctor?" for personal injury), but keep the first call short.

## Score the lead

| Category | Meaning | Next step |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Qualified | Right practice, area and timing | Book a consultation now |
| Maybe | Missing information | Collect documents, then attorney review |
| Not a fit | Wrong practice, area or no case | Refer out politely |
| Not a lead | Spam, sales calls, job seekers | Close it out |

## Refer out, do not just decline

A lead the firm cannot take is still a person who may remember how they were treated. Keep a short referral list of trusted firms and the state bar's lawyer referral service, and offer it. Follow your state's rules on referrals and referral fees.

## Fix the sources of bad leads

Bad leads usually come from a few fixable causes:

- **Ads too broad:** add negative keywords and narrow locations.
- **Pages that attract the wrong searches:** make practice pages clear about who you help and where.
- **Forms with no filter:** add a short "type of case" dropdown.
- **No source tracking:** without it you cannot tell which channel sends the bad leads.

## Track it monthly

Count leads by source and category each month. If one channel sends mostly "not a fit" leads, change the targeting before adding budget.
